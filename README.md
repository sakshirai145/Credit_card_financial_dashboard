# 💳 Credit Card Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Credit Card Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze credit card customer behavior, transaction patterns, revenue generation, and financial performance.

The project consists of two interactive report pages — **Credit Card Customer Report** and **Credit Card Transaction Report** — designed to transform raw financial data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, KPI cards, filters, and data-driven analysis.

The dashboard helps stakeholders understand customer demographics, spending behavior, transaction preferences, revenue distribution, and quarterly performance.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall credit card revenue, transaction amounts, and interest earned.
- Understand customer demographics based on age, gender, education, marital status, and occupation.
- Evaluate transaction behavior across different card categories and transaction methods.
- Identify revenue trends across quarters and weeks.
- Compare customer spending patterns across different income groups and expenditure categories.
- Present actionable business insights through interactive dashboards.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Calculated measures and KPI development.
- **Data Modeling** – Organizing data for analytical reporting.

**Key Visualizations:**

This report analyzes transaction performance, spending behavior, and card utilization.

- Total Interest Earned: 7.98M

**Key Visualizations:**
- Quarterly Revenue and Transaction Count
- Revenue by Transaction Method (Swipe, Chip, Online)

- Revenue by Education Level
- Revenue by Customer Job

- Analysis by Card Category, Income Group, Gender, and Quarter

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

The dashboard provides several useful observations:

- **Card Category:** Blue credit cards contribute the highest revenue compared to other card categories.
- **Customer Segmentation:** Businessmen contribute significantly to overall customer revenue.
- **Transaction Preferences:** Swipe transactions account for the largest share of transaction revenue.
- **Education Analysis:** Graduate customers represent a significant portion of revenue generation.
- **Quarterly Performance:** Revenue remains relatively consistent across all four quarters, with Q4 showing the highest transaction count.
- **Expenditure Analysis:** Bills represent the largest expenditure category in the transaction report.
- **Customer Demographics:** Middle-aged customer groups contribute substantially to overall revenue.
